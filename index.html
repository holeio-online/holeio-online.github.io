How to Play Hole io game

  1. Start the Match: Click Play to join a live match. You will be placed on a map with other players.
  2. Move Your Hole: Use your mouse (or finger on mobile) to drag and guide your black hole. The hole moves toward the cursor or touch point.
  3. Consume Objects: Swallow smaller objects first — pedestrians, trees, cars, and small structures — to grow in size. Bigger objects require a larger hole.
  4. Grow Strategically: Focus on clusters of objects or abandoned corners of the map early on. Avoid larger opponents until you’re big enough to swallow them.
  5. Use the Map & Timer: Watch the minimap and remaining time. Matches are short — usually a few minutes — so prioritize quick growth and efficient feeding.
  6. Dominate the Leaderboard: The bigger your hole, the higher you climb on the leaderboard. Last longer and keep consuming to stay at the top.
  7. Power-Ups & Respawn: When you grow large, you become a target; use movement and map awareness to survive. If you are eliminated, you can respawn and play again in the next round.

Tips & Tricks

  • Early game: stick to dense object clusters to grow fast.
  • Mid game: avoid head-on collisions with slightly larger holes — flank them instead.
  • Late game: control the center and absorb buildings to secure top leaderboard spots.
  • Mobile play: use short, controlled swipes to keep precise movement.
